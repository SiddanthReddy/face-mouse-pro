# Face Mouse Pro — 60-90 Second Showcase Demo Script & Storyboard

## 🎬 Video Overview
- **Goal**: Demonstrate seamless, hands-free computer control with zero latency, robust click disambiguation, and smooth navigation.
- **Target Duration**: 75 seconds (1:15).
- **Target Audience**: AI/CV recruiters, engineering managers, open-source community, assistive tech evaluators.
- **Recommended Free Recording Tools**:
  - **Screen Recording**: [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/) (1080p60, CQP/CRF 18)
  - **GIF Snippet Maker**: [ScreenToGif](https://www.screentogif.com/)
  - **Video Editing**: CapCut Desktop / Clipchamp / DaVinci Resolve (Free)

## 🎙️ Spoken Voiceover Narration Script

> **[00:00 - 00:12] Intro & Calibration**
> *"Meet Face Mouse Pro — a production-grade accessibility system that gives you complete, real-time computer control using just facial movements and gestures. After launching, looking straight ahead and pressing space instantly calibrates our neutral rest position."*

> **[00:12 - 00:25] Fluid Cursor Motion**
> *"As I move my head, the adaptive One-Euro filter tracks my nose vector with sub-pixel precision. It completely eliminates high-frequency resting jitter while keeping rapid head turns instant and responsive at over 30 FPS."*

> **[00:25 - 00:38] Left & Double Clicks**
> *"Clicking is powered by Eye Aspect Ratio detection. A deliberate left-eye blink performs a left click. A rapid double blink performs a double click. Notice that my natural involuntary blinking never triggers accidental clicks thanks to dual-eye synchronization filtering."*

> **[00:38 - 00:50] Right Click & Drag-and-Drop**
> *"A right eye blink immediately opens the context menu. To drag and drop, I hold my left eye closed for 0.8 seconds to lock the mouse down, glide the file across the screen, and hold again to drop."*

> **[00:50 - 01:02] Mouth-Open Scrolling**
> *"Need to read a long document? Simply open your mouth slightly to activate scroll mode. Tilting up scrolls up, tilting down scrolls down — effortless reading with zero hand contact."*

> **[01:02 - 01:15] Modern UI & Extensibility**
> *"Pressing 'O' opens our dark-mode settings panel to tune sensitivity and thresholds in real-time. Face Mouse Pro is fully open source, tested with pytest, and compiled as a standalone Windows executable. Check out the link below!"*
