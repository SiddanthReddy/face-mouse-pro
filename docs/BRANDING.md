# Face Mouse Pro — Brand Identity & Design System

## 1. Brand Essence & Personality
- **Identity**: Clean, Cybernetic, Accessible, High-Performance AI.
- **Tone**: Professional, Empowering, Precision-Engineered.

---

## 3. Typography Hierarchy
- **Desktop UI & Settings**: `Segoe UI`, `SF Pro Display`, `Inter` (sans-serif)
- **OpenCV HUD Overlay**: `cv2.FONT_HERSHEY_SIMPLEX` (antialiased `cv2.LINE_AA`)
- **Code & Metrics**: `JetBrains Mono`, `Fira Code`, `Consolas`

---

## 4. Logo Concept
The **Face Mouse Pro** insignia integrates:
1. An outer circular targeting reticle representing spatial computer vision tracking.
2. An inner magenta nodal core symbolizing AI landmark precision.
3. Dual crosshairs denoting accurate X/Y planar mapping.
